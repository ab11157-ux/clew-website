Insights for business owners

Understand the process before making the decision.

Selling a business can feel unfamiliar, personal, and consequential. Clew helps owners understand what buyers need, what comes next, and how to move through the process with greater confidence.

Common concerns

The questions owners ask first.

Will a private equity buyer replace my employees?

Not every buyer has the same plan. Many acquire a business because of the team, customer relationships, and operating knowledge already in place. Clew works to connect owners with buyers whose strategy and expectations fit the business, then helps both parties discuss employees and leadership directly before a transaction advances.

What will happen to the company’s name and legacy?

Brand, culture, and legacy are valid transaction priorities. Some buyers preserve an existing identity; others integrate companies over time. We help surface what matters to the owner early so the right questions are addressed before expectations harden.

Will the process remain confidential?

Conversations begin discreetly. A buyer is introduced only after the owner grants permission and appropriate confidentiality agreements are in place.

What information will a buyer request?

Requests typically become more detailed as interest develops, moving from high-level company and financial information to supporting records during diligence. Clew clarifies what is being requested, why it matters, and how the information can be organized.

Does speaking with Clew mean I have committed to selling?

No. An initial conversation is an opportunity to understand the market and consider whether a particular buyer could be a fit. Timing may be immediate, years away, or not right at all.
